# Wesh Tuition Centre — FAQ (English)

## About Us
Wesh Tuition Centre is a dedicated learning centre helping students achieve academic excellence. We specialise in primary and secondary school subjects, offering personalised attention in small group classes.

## Subjects Offered
We offer ONLY the following 6 standard Malaysian national curriculum subjects:
- English
- Bahasa Melayu (Malay)
- Mathematics
- History (Sejarah)
- Geography (Geografi)
- RBT (Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi)

*(Note: We DO NOT offer Additional Mathematics / Form 4 & 5 Add Maths, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Science, Accounting, or any IGCSE Computer Science / Cambridge subjects).*

## Subjects NOT Offered
We strictly DO NOT offer:
- Additional Mathematics (Form 4 and Form 5 Add Maths)
- Chemistry (Kimia), Physics (Fizik), Biology (Biologi), or Science (Sains)
- Principles of Accounting (Prinsip Perakaunan) or Economics
- Any IGCSE, O-Level, or A-Level subjects (including IGCSE Computer Science). We only support standard national curriculum (KSSR/KSSM).

## Fees & Packages

### Registration Fee
- There is a one-time registration fee of RM30 for new students, which includes a starter kit.

### Individual Subject Fees
- RM50 per subject per month. (We do not offer Add Maths).

### Package 1 — Language & Math (3 subjects)
- English + Bahasa Melayu + Mathematics
- RM100 per month (save RM50)

### Package 2 — Humanities (3 subjects)
- History + Geography + RBT
- RM100 per month (save RM50)

### Package 3 — All Subjects (6 subjects)
- English + Bahasa Melayu + Mathematics + History + Geography + RBT
- RM170 per month (save RM130)

All fees are charged monthly and due by the 5th of each month.

### Discounts & Promotions
- We do not offer a 50% discount if you bring 3 students. 
- We offer a 10% sibling discount for additional children enrolled under the same parent.

## Class Schedule & Operating Hours
- Monday to Friday: 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed (We have NO classes on Sundays. Sunday classes are not offered).

Classes are 1 hour per subject per session.

## Trial Class
We offer a FREE trial class for new students. No commitment required. Book a trial to experience our teaching style before enrolling.

## Location
Wesh Tuition Centre is located in Melaka. Free parking is available right outside the centre.

## Class Size
We maintain small group classes of maximum 10 students to ensure personal attention from teachers.

- To enrol, book a free trial class via WhatsApp.
