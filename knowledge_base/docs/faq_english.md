# Wesh Tuition Centre — FAQ (English)

## About Us
Wesh Tuition Centre is a dedicated learning centre helping students achieve academic excellence. We specialise in primary and secondary school subjects, offering personalised attention in small group classes.

## Subjects Offered
We offer the following subjects:
- English
- Bahasa Melayu (Malay)
- Mathematics
- History (Sejarah)
- Geography (Geografi)
- RBT (Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi)

## Fees & Packages

### Individual Subject
- RM50 per subject per month

### Package 1 — Language & Math (3 subjects)
- English + Bahasa Melayu + Mathematics
- RM100 per month (save RM50)

### Package 2 — Humanities (3 subjects)
- History + Geography + RBT
- RM100 per month (save RM50)

### Package 3 — All Subjects (6 subjects)
- English + Bahasa Melayu + Mathematics + History + Geography + RBT
- RM170 per month (save RM130)

All fees are charged monthly and due by the 5th of each month.

## Class Schedule
- Monday to Friday: 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed

Classes are 1 hour per subject per session.

## Trial Class
We offer a FREE trial class for new students. No commitment required. Book a trial to experience our teaching style before enrolling.

## Location
Wesh Tuition Centre is located in Melaka. Exact address will be provided upon booking confirmation.

## Class Size
We maintain small group classes of maximum 10 students to ensure every student gets personal attention from the teacher.

## Teachers
Our teachers are experienced, qualified, and passionate educators. Each subject is taught by a dedicated specialist teacher.

## Enrolment
To enrol, simply book a trial class via WhatsApp. After the trial, our team will guide you through the registration process. Required documents: copy of student's IC and latest report card.

## Payment Methods
We accept cash and online bank transfer (TNG, DuitNow).

## Contact
WhatsApp us anytime to book a trial class, ask questions, or get more information. Our team responds promptly during office hours (9 AM – 6 PM).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What age group do you accept?**
A: We accept students from Year 1 to Form 5 (ages 7–17).

**Q: Can my child take only one subject?**
A: Yes! Students can enrol in as little as one subject at RM50/month.

**Q: Is the trial class really free?**
A: Yes, the first trial class is completely free with no obligation to continue.

**Q: What if my child misses a class?**
A: We offer one replacement class per month for missed sessions, subject to availability.

**Q: Do you provide study materials?**
A: Yes, we provide worksheets and exercise materials. Students should bring their own stationery.

**Q: Can I pay for multiple months in advance?**
A: Yes, we accept advance payment. Please speak to our admin for details.

**Q: Is there a registration fee?**
A: There is a one-time registration fee of RM30 for new students, which includes a starter kit.
